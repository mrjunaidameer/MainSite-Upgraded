Where It All Started

Falling in Love with Science & Tech

Ever since I can remember, I've loved exploring science and learning how technology works. While studying cybersecurity, I started sharing that excitement through content.

The Turning Point

Starting Fresh in English

I originally built my initial audience in my local language. But as I grew, I wanted to share my work with people everywhere. I made the big decision to delete my old local accounts and restart completely in English so anyone in the world could follow along.

Two Distinct Passions

Dedicated Spaces for Topics

Because I love both cutting-edge technology and ancient nature mysteries, I created two dedicated spaces: Junaid Ameer for Tech/Space, and JUNIREX for Nature/Prehistoric Wildlife.
